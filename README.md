# Why this Repo?
My digital card company started seeing some growth when my Dad talked to his boss about the cards and the company was interested!

They had a business card template that I was able to mimick/modernize in Inkscape, but the problem was that each card required specific edits in the template (updating names, emails, and cell phone numbers). I'd lose a lot of time manually updating each template. Creating digital cards require five steps:

1. Designing/printing card designs
2. Adhering design to the card
3. Programming the card
4. Packaging the card
5. Shipping to Customer

Step 1 took too long for an order of that magnitude, so built this repo to house the tools that were designed to help save time in the process.
